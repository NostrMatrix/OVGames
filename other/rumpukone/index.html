Sample kit

Each instrument plays either its synth sound or your samples (WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC…). Drop files on an instrument row (here or in the grid) or on this panel to auto-assign by name (kick, snare, tom 1/2/3 or high/mid/floor, hihat closed/open, ride, crash 1/2, splash, china). Several files per instrument: similar loudness → round robin; clearly different loudness → velocity layers (light/normal/accent pick softer/harder samples). Samples are stored in this browser and are not included in song JSON files.

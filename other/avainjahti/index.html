How this works: each cycle draws 30 private keys with the browser's cryptographic random number generator, derives their public keys on the secp256k1 curve and from those the legacy (P2PKH) and bech32 (P2WPKH) addresses, and looks up the balances. All keys are computed locally in the browser — only the finished addresses are sent over the network.
Reality check: the address space is 2160 and there are ~5 × 107 funded addresses. The probability of a hit per address is therefore about 1 : 1040. This page is an illustration of the size of the key space, not a working search method.
Balance source: blockchain.info (batched), with mempool.space as a fallback. The free APIs have rate limits — if you get 429 errors, increase the refresh interval or reduce the batch size.
